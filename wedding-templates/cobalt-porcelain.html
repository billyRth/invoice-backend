Sophea & Elias

We are getting married by the river, and we would love you there for the whole weekend.

RSVP See the day

Together with our families, we invite you to celebrate our marriage.

We met in a queue for a broken ferry in 2019 and have been travelling badly together ever since. In April we would like to stop travelling for two days, stand under a tamarind tree with the people we love, and eat far too much.

There is no gift table. Come, eat, dance, and stay late.
